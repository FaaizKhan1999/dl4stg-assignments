# Q2.11 Prediction

## Model B 

It is trained with mirror and rotation augmentation, and will reduce $C_M$, $C_R$, $E_M$ and $E_R$, especially for rotation, because those gaps come from unfamiliar digit appearance, but it will not help at 2× or 3× since it still only sees 1× motion.

## Model C

It is trained on 2× and 3× windows, will fix 2×, where motion is mostly within the 8-pixel reach and the failure is due to unfamiliar speed, but will still do poorly at 3× because those displacements exceed what the architecture can carry in one step.

## Model D

It has a larger reach and the same speed augmentation, and will also fix 3x.
