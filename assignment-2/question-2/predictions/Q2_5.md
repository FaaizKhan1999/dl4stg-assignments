# Q2.5 Prediction

## Mirror and rotation

I predict model A will mostly pass the mirror test and do somewhat worse on the rotation test. From Figure 1(a), all 18 directions have motion and no direction is missing or rare, so every direction that a mirrored or rotated video produces is already well represented in training. The motion itself should be familiar, and digits should still move in the correct direction. We expect any errors to come from appearance: a mirrored/rotated digit is not a real digit, but rotated digits should seem less familiar than mirrored ones. I therefore expect $C_M$ and $E_M$ only slightly above $E^{(5)}$ at 1×, and $C_R$ and $E_R$ larger, with errors showing up as blurred or distorted digit shapes rather than wrong motion.

## Speed

My reach estimate is 8 pixels per step per axis (2 convolutions × 1 cell × 4 pixels per cell). From Figure 1(b), motion is concentrated between 3 and 4.5 pixels per frame (median 3.32). So 1× (3–4.5 px) is well within reach. 2× (6–9 px) straddles the 8-pixel limit and is unseen in training, so I expect noticeable degradation but not complete failure. 3× (9–13.5 px) is beyond reach and unseen in training, so I predict model A breaks down at 3×: digits will blur, stall or vanish instead of moving to the right place. Because the reach is per axis, diagonal motion at 3× may degrade somewhat less than motion along the axes.
